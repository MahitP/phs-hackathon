About this data

Air data comes from the CAMS model through Open-Meteo. Its grid is about 40 km wide, so nearby squares often share a value; street-level differences need ground sensors. Fire points are VIIRS thermal detections with low-confidence ones removed. They include small crop and brush burns and some industrial heat sources, not only wildfires.

About the Earth view

How to use it

Drag to turn the globe, scroll or pinch to zoom, and tap any spot to set its air beside PHS. Red numbers in the table are dirtier than PHS, green ones cleaner.

Where the numbers come from

The CAMS atmospheric model via Open-Meteo, updated hourly. Model estimates can differ from local ground monitors; airnow.gov has station readings for the United States.

Sunbeam through the haze

T = e−AOD / sin(sun elevation)

The Beer–Lambert law: the lower the sun and the thicker the haze, the less of the direct beam reaches the window. About 96% at noon on October 6. About 9% at 5 pm on June 7, 2023.

Honest about the sensor

The Grove sensor measures VOC gas relative to the room's own clean-air baseline. So SkyWindow shows a stale index from 0 to 100, never a made-up indoor AQI. The outdoor AQI comes from real atmospheric data.

Trained on our own readings

We labeled sensor data during the hackathon: normal air, cleaning sprays and markers, breath, and a covered light sensor. A random forest learned to tell them apart, tested on sessions it never saw.

Real alerts

A red banner appears when the air over PHS is actually unhealthy, with the EPA's health advice. An orange fire watch appears when NASA satellites see sizeable fires upwind; an open verdict then softens to either way.
