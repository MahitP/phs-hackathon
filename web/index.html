    HOW IT WORKS

    Sky → Device → Decision

    1. 01 / SENSEInside the school. A Grove air-quality sensor (VOC gas) and a light sensor facing the window, read by a Raspberry Pi through a Grove Base HAT.
    2. 02 / SKY LINKOutside. Live US AQI, PM2.5, ozone, aerosol optical depth (smoke haze), weather and wind for our exact coordinates, plus NASA satellite fire detections near PHS. Sizeable fires upwind trigger a Fire Watch.
    3. 03 / PHYSICSSunbeam through haze. Beer–Lambert law: T = e−AOD / sin(sun elevation), about 88% on a clear day and about 14–30% under 2023's smoke. The light sensor is calibrated against the clear-sky sun (Haurwitz model).
    4. 04 / DECIDEOne verdict. Sky vs. room → OPEN EITHER SHUT VENTILATE, shown on the RGB LCD.
    5. 05 / ANNOUNCEOne sentence, out loud. Gemini turns the verdict into a short classroom announcement (facts only) and ElevenLabs reads it aloud. A random-forest classifier we trained on our own labeled sensor data recognizes indoor events.

    HONESTY NOTE: The indoor sensor is a relative VOC gas sensor. We show a "stale index" compared to the room's own baseline, never a fake AQI.
